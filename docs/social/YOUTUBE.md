# YouTube: @TardiGeddon

Everything to set up the channel and its first month of videos. Shared names,
bios and rules are in [README.md](README.md); pictures are in
`store/social/`.

Most settings are in **YouTube Studio** (studio.youtube.com) → Settings or
→ Customisation.

## 1. The account

- **Brand Account.** Check the channel is a Brand Account (YouTube →
  Settings → "Add or manage your channels" shows it as a separate channel).
  Then Studio → Settings → Permissions → invite your personal Google account
  as **Owner**, so the channel isn't lost if one login is.
- **Two-step sign-in** on every Google account that has access.
- **Verify the channel** with a phone number (youtube.com/verify). It unlocks
  custom thumbnails, videos over 15 minutes and live streams.

## 3. Settings

Studio → **Settings**:

- **General:** currency GBP.
- **Channel → Basic info:** country **United Kingdom**. Keywords:
  `TardiGeddon "tardigrade game" "artillery game" "turn-based strategy" "browser game" "free online game" "multiplayer game" "indie game" "party game" tardigrades "water bear"`
  (never "Worms" or other games' names: README "Rules for every post").
- **Channel → Audience:** **"No, set this channel as not made for kids"**,
  and still answer per video. Reasoning: the channel is for a general and
  teen audience (online accounts are 13+), and the videos are game trailers,
  guides and dev diaries rather than content aimed at children. But
  US law (COPPA) judges each video by its content and intended audience, and
  cartoon characters count towards "made for kids", so **don't** make videos
  aimed at young children (nursery-style songs, "for kids" titles, toy-style
  play). If you ever do, mark that video "made for kids". Getting this wrong
  can bring fines, so if in doubt ask a lawyer.
- **Channel → Advanced:** leave "Allow YouTube to display ads" for later
  (monetisation needs 1,000 subscribers anyway). Note the game promises no
  ads; ads on YouTube videos are YouTube's, not in the game, but some players
  may not see the difference.
- **Upload defaults** (saves retyping on every video):
  - Title: `| TardiGeddon`
  - Description: the template in §6
  - Tags: `TardiGeddon, tardigrade game, artillery game, turn-based, browser game, free game, indie game, multiplayer`
  - Visibility: Private (publish by hand or schedule)
  - Category: **Gaming**. Video language and title language: English (United Kingdom)
  - Caption certification: "This content has never aired on television in the US"
  - Altered content: **No** (cartoon game footage isn't realistic)
  - Comments: **On**, hold potentially inappropriate comments for review
  - Licence: Standard YouTube Licence
  - Game title: type "TardiGeddon". YouTube only lists games it knows; if it
    isn't there yet, leave it blank and try again after launch coverage.
- **Community → Automated filters:** blocked words `free nitro, robux, giveaway, telegram, whatsapp, crypto` and "Block links": on. Add your personal account as a **Moderator**.

## 7. On every video

- **Thumbnail:** custom, 1280×720, three to five words of huge text, one tardi
  big, one clear moment. Copy the layout of `youtube-thumb-template.png`
  (title top-left, logo bottom-left, a tardi on the right) so the channel
  looks like a set. Fonts: Luckiest Guy for the words, the game's yellow
  `#ffd84a` and red `#e04848` with the ink outline `#2b1b24`.
- **End screen** (last 5–20 s): "Subscribe" + "Best for viewer" video, and
  a playlist. **Cards:** link the How to play video from anything a beginner
  might watch.
- **Chapters:** start the description's chapters at `0:00`; three or more
  make YouTube show them.
- **Captions:** check YouTube's automatic ones and fix names (TardiGeddon,
  tardi, Spore Bazooka). Then add translated titles and descriptions
  (Studio → Subtitles → Add language) for Spanish, Portuguese, German, French
  and Japanese, since the game speaks those too.
- **First hour:** reply to every comment. It's the biggest boost a new
  channel gets.

## 8. Recording

- **Software:** OBS Studio (free). Canvas 1920×1080, 60 fps, record to MP4/MKV.
  For Shorts, a second scene at 1080×1920 with the game in the middle (like
  the teaser).
- **The game:** a desktop browser at 1920×1080, full screen. Useful links
  (CLAUDE.md "Commands"): `tardigeddon.com/play/?autostart=cpu` (straight
  into a match vs the computer), `&players=10`, `&style=chaos`, `&map=sky`,
  `&seed=123` (the same map again, for retakes).
- **Replays:** the game keeps replays, so play first, then record the replay
  for the clean shot without the pressure.
- **Sound:** game sound on, music low. Voice: any USB mic, close to your mouth.
- **Website clips:** `site/capture/capture-media.mjs` already records short,
  clean clips from the game (that's where the teaser came from); a developer
  can add more scenes to it for trailer shots.

## 9. Growing it

- Share every video in Discord `#announcements` and on the other socials as
  they go live.
- Email the YouTubers and streamers in docs/SEO.md "Press and creators": a
  private room for them and their friends is the best pitch. Point them to
  the press kit for clips and art.
- Check Studio → Analytics weekly: which thumbnails get clicked
  (impressions click-through rate; 4–10% is good) and where people stop
  watching. Make more of what works.
