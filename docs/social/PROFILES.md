# Profile text for the other platforms

Bios and "About" text, ready to paste, for the platforms that don't have a
guide of their own. Discord, YouTube, TikTok, Bluesky and Reddit have theirs
([README.md](README.md) table). The shared bios, voice and rules are in
[README.md](README.md); everything here keeps to them: no "Worms", money only
buys looks, British spelling. Character counts are given where a platform has
a limit, and every text fits.

**Links:** the game first (`https://tardigeddon.com/play`), then the website.
Where a platform allows several links, add Discord (`tardigeddon.com/discord`)
and YouTube. Never link Patreon from inside the apps (see
[../PATREON.md](../PATREON.md) §1); profiles on social sites are fine.

## Pinterest

1. Sign up at pinterest.com with `developer@tardigeddon.com` as a **business
   account** (or convert: Settings → Account management → Convert to a
   business account). Business type: **Brand**; category: **Games** (or
   Entertainment). Two-factor authentication on.
2. **Settings → Claimed accounts → Websites → Claim** `tardigeddon.com`.
   Choose the **TXT record** option and add it in Cloudflare (tardigeddon.com
   → DNS → Records), like the Bluesky one; don't delete it later. A claimed
   site puts our logo on every pin from it and unlocks analytics.
3. **Edit profile:**
   - **Photo:** `store/social/youtube-avatar.png`
   - **Name** (30 at most): `TardiGeddon: Tardigrade Game` (28)
   - **Username:** `tardigeddon`
   - **About** (500 at most). Pinterest is a search engine, so this uses the
     words people search for (water bears, browser game):
     > TardiGeddon is a free turn-based artillery game starring tardigrades,
     > the tiny water bears that survive almost anything. Walk, aim and fire
     > across destructible maps, mind the wind, and be the last tardi
     > standing. Here: every weapon, map styles, game tips and real
     > tardigrade facts. Play free in your browser on computers, phones and
     > tablets. No download, no ads, nothing to buy that helps you win.

     (397)
   - **Website:** `https://tardigeddon.com/play`
4. Make the six boards in [SCHEDULE.md](SCHEDULE.md) ("Pinterest boards")
   before connecting Pinterest to Sociamonials.
