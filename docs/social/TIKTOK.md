# TikTok: @tardigeddon

Everything to set up the account and keep it going. Shared names, bios and
rules are in [README.md](README.md); pictures are in `store/social/`.

TikTok is where a game clip has the best chance of reaching people who've
never heard of us, so it gets the same Shorts as YouTube, posted natively
(never a YouTube link or a watermarked download).

## 1. The account

1. Install TikTok on the phone you'll post from, then **Sign up → Use phone
   or email → Email**: `developer@tardigeddon.com` (README "Names").
   Birthday: the account holder's real one (18+).
2. **Username:** `tardigeddon` (TikTok has no capitals). If taken,
   `tardigeddongame`, then `playtardigeddon`.
3. **Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Account → Switch to Business
   Account.** Category: **Games** (or Entertainment). A company account must
   be a business one under TikTok's terms, and it adds analytics and, for
   most accounts, a website link in the bio.
   The catch: business accounts only get the **Commercial Music Library**
   (sounds cleared for brands), not chart songs. That's what our rules want
   anyway (README "Music and clips").
4. **Settings and privacy → Security → 2-step verification:** on, with an
   authenticator app plus email.

## 3. Settings

Settings and privacy:

- **Privacy:** Private account **off**. Suggest your account to others:
  **on** for everything. Downloads: **on** (people sharing our clips is
  free advertising). Duet and Stitch: **Everyone**.
- **Comments:** allow from **Everyone**. Comment filters: **Filter spam and
  offensive comments: on**, and **Filter keywords: on** with
  `free nitro, robux, giveaway, telegram, whatsapp, bitcoin, airdrop, onlyfans`
  (not "crypto": it catches *cryptobiosis*).
- **Direct messages:** **No one**, or "Suggested friends" at most. Bug
  reports and business go to support@tardigeddon.com; DMs on TikTok mostly
  bring scams.
- **Mentions and tags:** Everyone (people tagging us is good).

## 4. Posting

Post the same vertical clips as YouTube Shorts (`store/social/shorts/`), but
**upload the original file**, not a download from YouTube: TikTok hides
videos with another platform's watermark.

Each post, in the TikTok app:

1. **+ → Upload** → the clip.
2. **Add sound** → pick something from the Commercial Music Library that
   fits (upbeat, comic, or a sound that's trending). Keep the game's own
   explosions audible; lower the music if needed.
3. **Text on screen** for the first second, the hook: e.g. *"tardigrades with
   bazookas"*, *"he survived THAT?"*, *"worst shot of the day"*.
4. **Caption:** one line, then 3–5 hashtags:
   ```
   Tiny tardigrades, huge explosions 💥 Free in your browser, link in bio
   #TardiGeddon #indiegame #browsergame #gaming #tardigrade
   ```
5. **More options → AI-generated content: off.** It's for realistic AI
   footage; cartoon game footage doesn't need it (README "AI").
6. **Who can watch:** Everyone. Allow comments, Duet, Stitch.
7. Post in the evening UK time (6–9 pm) to start with; TikTok Analytics
   (Business suite) shows when followers are online once there are some.

**First post:** the teaser (`shorts/teaser.mp4`), with the caption above.

## 5. What works on TikTok

- **The first second decides everything.** Start on the explosion, the
  near-miss or the tardi flying, never on a menu or a logo.
- **7–20 seconds** for most posts. One moment per video.
- **Post often:** 3–5 a week beats one polished video. Recycle: the same
  match gives a "best shot", a "worst miss" and a "Concrete Tun" clip.
- **Series** people follow: *Weapon of the day*, *Daily challenge in 15
  seconds*, *Rate my shot 1–10*, *Things you didn't know tardigrades can
  survive* (real facts, with a source in the caption).
- **Reply to comments with a video** ("Reply with video" on a comment): the
  most-asked questions make easy posts.
- **Trends:** use a trending sound or format when it fits a game moment;
  don't force it.
- Never "Worms" in captions or hashtags (README rules), and nothing aimed at
  young children: TikTok is 13+ and the account is for teens and adults.
