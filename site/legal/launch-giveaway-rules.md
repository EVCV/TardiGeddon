## Section 1 — The Promoter

1.0.1 The Giveaway is run by EVCV Limited, whose details are above. The entry page is hosted for us by Sociamonials, our giveaway service provider.

## Section 2 — No Purchase Necessary

2.0.1 Entry is free. No purchase or payment of any kind is needed to enter or to win, and making a purchase does not increase your chances of winning.

2.0.2 You do not need to download any app, or rate or review anything, to enter or to earn bonus entries.

## Section 3 — Who Can Enter

3.0.1 The Giveaway is open to anyone aged **13 or over** on the day they enter.

3.0.2 If you are under 18 (or under the age of majority where you live, if that is higher), you must have permission from a parent or legal guardian before you enter, and they must agree to these rules on your behalf. We may ask for that permission to be confirmed before we give a prize (Section 8).

3.0.3 The Giveaway is open worldwide **except** to people who live in:

- the Province of Quebec, Canada;
- Italy;
- Brazil;
- Vietnam;
- any country or region where we cannot lawfully run the Giveaway or give a prize, including because of sanctions imposed by the United Kingdom, the European Union, the United States or the United Nations;

and it is void wherever it is prohibited or restricted by law.

3.0.4 Employees and directors of EVCV Limited, their immediate families (parents, children, brothers and sisters, and partners) and anyone involved in running the Giveaway cannot enter.

3.0.5 Each person can win only one prize.

## Section 4 — When It Runs

4.0.1 The Giveaway opens when we publish its entry page, which we will announce on our website and social media, and no earlier than 12 November 2026. It closes at **23:59 GMT on 31 January 2027** (the "Entry Period"). Entries received outside the Entry Period will not count. The time recorded by the entry page is the official time.

## Section 5 — How to Enter

5.0.1 Go to the entry page during the Entry Period, fill in the entry form with your real details (first name, email address and the country you live in), confirm the age and permission statement, and agree to these rules. Then confirm your entry using the link we email you. That gives you 1 entry.

5.0.2 After entering, you can earn **bonus entries** on the entry page, up to **25 bonus entries** in total per person:

- **Make a free TardiGeddon account** at https://tardigeddon.com/play and tell us your player name: 5 bonus entries. Accounts are free and are for ages 13 and over.
- **Invite friends**: 3 bonus entries for each friend who enters using your personal referral link and confirms their entry. Your friends must be eligible to enter themselves. Please only invite people you know, and don't post your link where it breaks a website's or app's rules.
- **Follow or subscribe to us** on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, X, Bluesky, Twitch and Reddit, follow TardiGeddon on itch.io, add it to your list on IGDB, and **join our Discord server** (ages 13 and over): 1 bonus entry each. Each of these sites has its own age rules; only use the ones you are old enough for.

5.0.3 One person, one entry. You may enter only once, with one email address. Entries made using more than one email address, false details, automated means (bots, scripts), entry-swapping groups, or any other method that gets around these rules will be disqualified, along with every entry from that person.

5.0.4 We are not responsible for entries that are lost, late, incomplete or not received because of technical problems, including problems with the entry page, email delivery or any social network.

6.0.3 **Second prize (5 winners), each:**

- the **Launch Champion set**, never sold: Champion's Arena (a world), Champion's Fanfare (a music track), Champion's crown (a hat), Champion's monument (a gravestone), Champion's sights (an aim style) and Champion's salute (an explosion style);

6.0.4 **Third prize (20 winners), each:**

6.0.6 Prizes are given to the winner's own TardiGeddon account, which is free to make (ages 13 and over). ## Section 7 — The Grand Prize Design Set

7.0.1 We will work with the grand prize winner by email only, in the six months after the prize is confirmed. There are no calls or meetings, and the winner does not need to travel anywhere. If the winner is under 18, a parent or guardian must agree in writing before we start and be copied in on our emails.

7.0.2 The winner shares ideas (for example a theme, colours, sketches and the mood of the music), and our team makes the final designs and the music. We have the final say on what is made, so that it fits the game and our Community Guidelines and stays fair for everyone. The finished items are cosmetic only.

7.0.3 By taking part, the winner (and, if they are under 18, their parent or guardian) agrees that EVCV Limited owns the finished designs, and gives us a free, worldwide, permanent licence to use any ideas, sketches or other material they share with us for the set, in the game, on our websites and in our marketing. The winner must only share material they made themselves. We will credit the winner by their player name only, never by their real name, unless they and (if under 18) their parent or guardian ask us in writing to use their real name.

7.0.4 If the winner chooses not to take part in designing the set, or cannot do so within six months, they still receive the rest of the grand prize, and we may finish the set ourselves or not make it.

## Section 8 — Choosing and Contacting Winners

8.0.1 Winners will be drawn at random from all eligible entries (each entry and bonus entry counting as one chance) within 7 days after the Entry Period closes, using the entry page's random selection. The first entry drawn wins the grand prize, the next five the second prizes, and the next twenty the third prizes. If an entry drawn belongs to someone who has already been drawn, or who is not eligible, another entry is drawn.

8.0.2 We will email each winner, at the address they entered with, within 14 days after the Entry Period closes. To claim the prize, the winner must reply within **14 days** of our email with:

- the country they live in, so we can check they can enter (Section 3);
- the player name or email address of the TardiGeddon account the prize should go to (or make a free account first);
- for a third prize, which Shop bundle they would like;
- if they are under 18, a short confirmation from a parent or guardian that they agree to these rules (and, for the grand prize, to Section 7).

8.0.3 If a winner does not reply in time, cannot be contacted, is not eligible, or does not meet these rules, they lose the prize and we may draw another winner for it.

8.0.4 We aim to give each prize within 30 days after the winner claims it.

9.0.2 To ask for the list of winners (player names and prizes), email support@tardigeddon.com within 3 months after the Entry Period closes.
