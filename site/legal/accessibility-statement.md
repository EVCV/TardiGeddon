## Introduction

We want as many people as possible to be able to enjoy TardiGeddon. This statement explains our current accessibility position, the measures we take, and, importantly, the fact that the Website and game are presently in active BETA development.

---

## Section 1 — Our Commitment to Accessibility

### 1.0 Accessibility Goals

1.0.1 The Company is committed to making the Website and game accessible to people of all abilities, including people with visual, auditory, motor, or cognitive disabilities. For the Website, we aim to conform, on a best-efforts and ongoing basis, to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), and to comply with our obligations, where applicable, under the Equality Act 2010.

1.0.2 We recognise that accessibility is an ongoing process rather than a one-time compliance exercise, and we are committed to continuous improvement over time.

### 1.1 Beta Status of the Website

1.1.1 **The Website and the game are currently operating in BETA.** This means their features, layout, and content are under continuous, active development, review, and revision, and have not yet undergone a complete, formal accessibility audit or certification against WCAG 2.1 AA or any other accessibility standard.

1.1.2 As the Website and game are in BETA, some pages, features, interactive elements, or third-party content may not yet fully conform to WCAG 2.1 AA or other applicable accessibility standards. Known and unknown accessibility barriers may exist as a result of the current stage of development.

1.1.3 We are actively working to identify, prioritise, and remediate accessibility issues as an ongoing part of our development process, and this Accessibility Statement will be updated as accessibility improves.

1.1.4 By using the Website and game during this BETA period, you acknowledge and accept that: (a) accessibility conformance is a work in progress; (b) the Company's failure to meet a particular accessibility standard, guideline, or success criterion at this stage does not constitute a waiver of, or admission regarding, the Company's accessibility commitments; and (c) the Company shall not be liable for any accessibility gap, barrier, or non-conformance identified or arising during the BETA period, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

1.1.5 The BETA designation does not diminish our commitment to accessibility improvement, nor does it exempt us from making reasonable adjustments where required by applicable disability discrimination law; rather, it reflects the current, evolving state of a Website and game under active development.

## Section 2 — Measures Taken to Support Accessibility

### 2.0 Ongoing Efforts

2.0.1 The Website respects your device's "reduce motion" setting: smooth scrolling and moving animations are switched off when it is on.

2.0.2 The game can be played with a keyboard, a mouse, or touch controls, and turn-based play means there is no need for fast reactions: each turn has a timer, and game styles with longer turn times can be chosen in the menu.

2.0.3 Teams are identified by name as well as by colour, and speech-bubble reactions in the game are shown as text.

2.0.4 Including accessibility considerations at the design and development stage of new features, so far as reasonably practicable given the BETA status, and conducting periodic internal reviews using automated testing tools and manual review.

2.0.5 Maintaining an accessible channel for players to report accessibility barriers, as set out in Section 3, and prioritising fixes by severity of impact.

## Section 3 — Feedback and Reporting Issues

### 3.0 How to Contact Us

3.0.1 We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of TardiGeddon. If you encounter any accessibility barriers, please contact us at support@tardigeddon.com, including: (a) the page or part of the game where the issue occurred; (b) a description of the issue you encountered; (c) the assistive technology, device, or browser you were using, if relevant; and (d) your contact details, if you would like a response.

3.0.2 We aim to acknowledge accessibility feedback within 5 business days, and to provide an update on our assessment and, where reasonably practicable, a timeline for a fix.

### 3.1 Alternative Access

3.1.1 If you are unable to access particular content on the Website due to an accessibility barrier, please contact us using the details above, and we will use reasonable endeavours to provide the information you need through an alternative means.

## Section 4 — Known Limitations

### 4.0 Current Limitations

4.0.1 As the Website and game are in active BETA development, known limitations may exist from time to time. These currently include:

- The game is drawn on a canvas, so the playing field (the landscape, tardigrades, and weapons) is not available to screen readers. The menus are standard web pages.
- The game has explosions, camera movement, and flashing effects, and does not yet have a reduced-motion or reduced-flashing option.
- Sound effects do not have captions, although the game does not rely on sound to play.
- Aiming and charging a shot require holding a button or key, which may be difficult for some players.

4.0.2 We will update this list as issues are identified and resolved.
