## Introduction

TardiGeddon is about blowing up cartoon tardigrades, not each other. These Community Guidelines explain how we expect players to behave in online matches, so that everyone, including younger players, has a good time.

---

## Section 1 — Our Community Values

### 1.0 Purpose

1.0.1 These Community Guidelines apply to online play, including quick play and private rooms (together, "Online Play"), and to the team and tardigrade names you choose, because other players see them.

1.0.2 These Guidelines supplement, and should be read alongside, our Terms of Service.

## Section 2 — Expected Behaviour

### 2.0 Be Respectful

2.0.1 Play fair and be a good sport, whether you win or lose. Harassment, hate speech, bullying, threats, or personal attacks directed at any individual or group are not tolerated.

### 2.1 Choose Friendly Names

2.1.1 Team and tardigrade names, and the player name on your account, must be suitable for players of all ages. Do not use names that are offensive, sexual, hateful, or that mock or target anyone.

2.1.2 Do not use your real full name, address, school, phone number, social media handle, or any other personal information in your names, and do not use other people's.

### 2.2 Be Honest

2.2.1 Do not impersonate other people, including TardiGeddon staff.

### 2.3 Play the Game

2.3.1 Do not deliberately stall, sabotage, or ruin matches for other players, for example by repeatedly joining and leaving rooms.

## Section 3 — Prohibited Behaviour

### 3.0 Not Allowed

3.0.1 Names or behaviour that are illegal, sexually explicit, discriminatory on the basis of a protected characteristic, or that promote self-harm.

3.0.2 Cheating, including modified game clients, tampering with network messages, bots that play for you against other people, and deliberately exploiting bugs.

3.0.3 Attacking or overloading the online server, including flooding it with connections, rooms, or messages.

3.0.4 Names that infringe the intellectual property rights of others, as further described in our Copyright Policy.

## Section 4 — Moderation

### 4.0 Our Right to Moderate

4.0.1 We may change or hide names, remove players from matches, and block access to Online Play for players who break these Guidelines, at our discretion and without prior notice where circumstances require.

### 4.1 Appeals

4.1.1 If you believe action was taken against you in error, you may appeal by contacting support@tardigeddon.com with relevant details. We will review appeals within 10 business days.

## Section 5 — Reporting

### 5.0 Reporting a Player

5.0.1 If another player breaks these Guidelines, please email support@tardigeddon.com with the room code, the team name, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

5.0.2 We review reports according to severity and take action consistent with Section 4. Because we do not keep match records, reports are most useful when sent soon after the match.

5.0.3 If you are worried about your safety or someone else's, please tell a trusted adult and, where appropriate, contact the police.
